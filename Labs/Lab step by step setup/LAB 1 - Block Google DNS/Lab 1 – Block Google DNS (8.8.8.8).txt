Lab 1 – Block Google DNS (8.8.8.8)

Step 1: Create an Address Object

In the FortiGate GUI:

Policy & Objects → Addresses → Create New

Configure:

Name: Google_DNS
Type: IP/Netmask
Subnet/IP: 8.8.8.8/32

Click OK.

Step 2: Create a Deny Policy

Go to:

Policy & Objects → Firewall Policy → Create New

Configure:

Name: Block_Google_DNS
Incoming Interface: port2
Outgoing Interface: port1
Source: all
Destination: Google_DNS
Schedule: always
Service: ALL
Action: Deny

Save it.

Step 3: Move the Policy to the Top

FortiGate checks policies from top to bottom.

Your deny rule must be above the allow rule.

The order should be:

❌ Block_Google_DNS
✅ LAN-to-Internet
Step 4: Test

From the Windows VM:

ping 8.8.8.8

It should fail.

Then try:

ping 1.1.1.1

It should still work.

This shows that only traffic matching the first rule is blocked.
