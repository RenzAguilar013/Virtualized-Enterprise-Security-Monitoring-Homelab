Lab: Block Internet Outside Business Hours
Step 1: Create a Schedule

In the GUI:

Policy & Objects → Schedules → Create New

Choose Recurring.

Example:

Name: Office_Hours
Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday
Time: 08:00–17:00

Click OK.

Step 2: Edit Your LAN-to-Internet Policy

Go to:

Policy & Objects → Firewall Policy

Edit your LAN-to-Internet policy.

Change:

Schedule: always

to

Schedule: Office_Hours

Leave everything else the same:

Source Interface: port2
Destination Interface: port1
Action: Accept
NAT: Enabled

Save the policy.

Step 3: Test It

If the current time is outside 08:00–17:00 on Monday–Friday:

❌ Websites should not load.
❌ ping 8.8.8.8 should fail.

During the configured schedule:

✅ Internet access should work normally.
