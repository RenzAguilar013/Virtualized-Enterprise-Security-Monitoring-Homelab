MITRE ATT&CK T1087.002 — Domain Account Discovery
Source endpoint: Renz / 10.10.10.20
Domain controller: DC01 / 10.10.10.10

Set the Threat Hunting time range to cover the test.

1. Exact native discovery command
agent.name:"Renz" AND data.win.system.eventID:"1" AND "net user /domain"

2. net.exe or net1.exe process activity
agent.name:"Renz" AND data.win.system.eventID:"1" AND (data.win.eventdata.image:*net.exe OR data.win.eventdata.image:*net1.exe)

3. Command-line search
agent.name:"Renz" AND data.win.system.eventID:"1" AND data.win.eventdata.commandLine:*user* AND data.win.eventdata.commandLine:*/domain*

4. PowerShell script-block discovery
agent.name:"Renz" AND data.win.system.eventID:"4104" AND ("Get-ADUser" OR "adsisearcher" OR "samaccountname")

5. Broad T1087.002 investigation
agent.name:"Renz" AND ("net user /domain" OR "Get-ADUser" OR "adsisearcher" OR "samaccountname")

Recommended columns:
- timestamp
- agent.name
- rule.id
- rule.level
- rule.description
- data.win.system.eventID
- data.win.eventdata.user
- data.win.eventdata.image
- data.win.eventdata.parentImage
- data.win.eventdata.commandLine
- data.win.eventdata.scriptBlockText
- full_log

If the events do not appear in wazuh-alerts-*, repeat the searches in
wazuh-archives-* when archives are enabled. Some collected process or PowerShell
events may not be promoted by the default Wazuh ruleset.
