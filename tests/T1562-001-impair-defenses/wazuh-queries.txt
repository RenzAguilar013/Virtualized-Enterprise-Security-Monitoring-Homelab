MITRE ATT&CK T1562.001 — Linux Impair Defenses
Wazuh Threat Hunting queries for Linux01

Set the time range to cover the test before running these DQL searches.

1. Broad Linux01 service and marker search
agent.name:"UBUNTU-10.10.10.50" AND ("wazuh-agent" OR "T1562_001_WAZUH_AGENT_STOPPED")

2. systemctl status and recovery activity
agent.name:"UBUNTU-10.10.10.50" AND "systemctl" AND ("wazuh-agent" OR "is-active")

3. auditd execution records for systemctl
agent.name:"UBUNTU-10.10.10.50" AND data.audit.command:"systemctl"

4. sudo activity involving the Wazuh Agent
agent.name:"UBUNTU-10.10.10.50" AND "sudo" AND "wazuh-agent"

5. Post-recovery agent events
agent.name:"UBUNTU-10.10.10.50" AND rule.groups:"syslog"

Recommended columns:
- timestamp
- agent.name
- rule.id
- rule.level
- rule.description
- full_log
- data.audit.exe
- data.audit.command
- data.audit.cwd
- data.audit.uid

Important observation:
The exact stop event or controlled marker may be absent from Wazuh because the
Wazuh Agent was the service being stopped and could not forward its own pending
events while impaired. Treat this as a documented telemetry gap. Use the auditd,
auth.log and systemd evidence forwarded independently to Splunk as the primary
evidence for the interruption, then use Wazuh for reconnection and post-recovery
validation.
